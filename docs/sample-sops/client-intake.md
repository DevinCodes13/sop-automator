# Client Intake Procedure

1. When a client walks in, the instructor greets them.
2. The instructor hands the client the sign-in clipboard right away.
3. The client writes their name, the number of haircuts, and whether they will pay with cash or card.
4. The instructor checks the student sign-in sheet and finds the student with the fewest haircuts today.
5. The instructor assigns the client to that student and adds a tally mark next to the student's name.
6. At the end of the day, the instructor files both sign-in sheets.
